Meet the animals

20 featured characters from the collection. Every design has a name and a story — and every Legendary is a different mythical animal, one of one.

Mochi

Common

Mochi is an orange tabby who naps in sunbeams and wakes up only for snacks. He once chased a ball of yarn all the way around Zigzoo and back, and he still insists he won.

Kiko

Rare

Kiko the red panda collects the prettiest leaves in Zigzoo and wears one on his head every day. He shares his bamboo with anyone who asks nicely — and with a few who don't.

Boba

Legendary

Boba the panda wears a golden crown because, as he puts it, someone has to be the king of naps. His bamboo is solid gold, his naps are legendary, and his smile is worth more than both.

Luna

Epic

Luna the purple owl carries a golden lantern through the night and lights the way home for anyone who is lost. Her light has never once gone out.

Daisy

Rare

Daisy the yellow duck wears a pink flower behind her ear and sings to the pond every morning. The pond has never once complained.

Pip

Rare

Pip the little green frog found a golden crown floating down the river and decided it was his all along. He rules his lily pad with a smile and a very loud croak.

Draco

Legendary

Draco is a baby dragon who wears his golden crown everywhere, even to bed. He guards the stars of Zigzoo and polishes each one until it shines.

Pyra

Legendary

Nova

Legendary

Gryff

Legendary
