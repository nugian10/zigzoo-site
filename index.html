04 / About

What Is ZIGZOO?

Experimental Collection

ZIGZOO is a collection of weird, unique, playful, and original cartoon characters. Each monster has a different body shape, eyes, mouth, antennae, and pattern — but they all feel like they come from one world.

Its visual language is deliberately like a vintage cartoon print: rough ink, grain, and imperfect printing, on a warm cream background. Flat 2D, with no glossy 3D effects.

1,111 Characters

Supply is hard-capped in the smart contract. There is no additional minting after 1,111 is reached, and no mint limit per wallet for authorized agents.

Agent Only

Minting is designed for Binance Agentic Wallet and compatible agents already authorized on BNB Chain. This is not a conventional public mint.

  • Silhouettes: blob, tall, square, triangle, ghost, dome, worm, cat with ears, diamond, strange flower, and spiky star.
  • Eyes: one large yellow/white eye, a vertical slit eye, or an unusual stalk-eye arrangement.
  • Details: antennae, horns, spikes, tails, stripes, dots, and zigzag or checkered teeth mouths.
