Mint with your AI agent

Zigzoo is agent-only: there is no mint button on this page. Give the prompt below to your AI agent (for example your Binance Agentic Wallet agent) and it will mint for you when the contract is live.

How it works

  1. Your agent reads this page and the skill file (agent-skill.md).
  2. It checks the mint is open and your wallet holds a SPACE ID Agent ID.
  3. It reads the exact price for your next mint from the contract.
  4. It shows you a preview — including the exact price — and only mints after you confirm.
  5. It verifies the mint on-chain and tells you which animal you got.

Prompt for your agent

Copy everything in the box and paste it to your agent:

You are an AI agent minting a Zigzoo NFT for your user. Zigzoo is a
collection of 5,000 cute animal character NFTs (ERC-721, symbol ZIGZOO)
on BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56). There are 590 designs: Common 400
designs x 10 editions (4,000 tokens), Rare 70 x 10 (700), Epic 20 x 10
(200), and Legendary 100 designs x 1 edition each (100) — every Legendary
is a different mythical animal. Images and metadata are public at
https://zigzoo.xyz/images/ and https://zigzoo.xyz/metadata/.

CONTRACT STATUS: NOT DEPLOYED YET. The contract address is
0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000 (placeholder). Do NOT attempt
any mint transaction until the Zigzoo site shows a real deployed contract
address. When the contract is live, this prompt and the site will show it.

WHEN LIVE — FACTS:
- Contract: <deployed address, shown on https://zigzoo.xyz/>
- SPACE ID Agent ID gate (registry 0x60B9262456dE011F7d15a54CdD8c48bAaa8B022F):
  only a wallet holding a Binance Agent ID can mint. The contract checks
  holdsAgentId on-chain. Contract calls are rejected (NoContracts).
  There is no owner allowlist.
- mint() takes NO arguments and mints exactly 1 NFT per call.
- Price ladder per wallet (public): mint #1 FREE, #2-10: 0.0002 BNB,
  #11-20: 0.0004 BNB, #21-30: 0.0008 BNB. Max 30 mints per wallet.
  Max total per wallet: 0.0138 BNB. Read priceFor(your wallet) or
  currentPrice() on the contract to get the exact price for the next mint
  — never hardcode a price. Any overpayment is refunded automatically,
  and paid mint value is forwarded directly to the revenue wallet.
- Minting is owner-controlled (mintActive). The draw is a shuffled deck
  (deckHash on-chain); each mint draws the next token. Rarity is rolled
  by the deck: Common 80%, Rare 14%, Epic 4%, Legendary 2%.

WHEN LIVE — FLOW, per mint:
1. Read totalSupply() (selector 0x18160ddd) and mintActive()
   (0x25fd90f3). If totalSupply is 5000, it is sold out. If mintActive
   is false, minting is not open yet.
2. Read holdsAgentId(your wallet) (0x11213086 + wallet padded to 32
   bytes). If false, the wallet cannot mint.
3. Read mintCount(your wallet) (0xed9ec888 + wallet padded to 32
   bytes). If it is 30, the wallet has reached its limit.
4. Read priceFor(your wallet) (0xf12a7add + wallet padded to 32 bytes)
   — that is the exact value to send for this mint.
5. Preview:
   baw contract-call preview --binanceChainId 56 --from <agentic wallet>
   --to <contract> --value <priceFor in wei> --inputData 0x1249c58b --json
6. Show the preview to the user, including the exact price. On their
   confirmation, execute with the requestId from the preview.
7. Verify on-chain: totalSupply() rose by 1 and mintCount() rose by 1.
   Read tokenURI(new tokenId) and designOf(new tokenId)
   (0xcbfd8d37 + tokenId padded to 32 bytes) to report the design,
   edition and rarity to the user.

Repeat as needed, up to 30 mints per wallet. Do not mint for a wallet
that does not hold an Agent ID, and never send more than priceFor().

Questions

When can I mint?
Not yet — the contract is being deployed. This page will show the real contract address and the mint status as soon as it is live.
How much does it cost?
Your first mint is free. After that the price rises per wallet: 0.0002, 0.0004, then 0.0008 BNB. See the price ladder above.
How is the animal chosen?
Every mint draws the next token from a shuffled deck, recorded on-chain as a deck hash. The draw is random by deck order, not by choice — your animal is a surprise.
Can I mint from a browser wallet?
No. Minting is agent-only by design: only a wallet holding a SPACE ID Agent ID can mint, and the mint must be made by an AI agent for its user.
