04 / About

What Is ZIGZOO?

Experimental Collection

ZIGZOO is a collection of weird, unique, playful, and original cartoon characters. Each monster has a different body shape, eyes, mouth, antennae, and pattern — but they all feel like they come from one world.

Its visual language is deliberately like a vintage cartoon print: rough ink, grain, and imperfect printing, on a warm cream background. Flat 2D, with no glossy 3D effects.

1,111 Characters

Supply is hard-capped in the smart contract. There is no additional minting after 1,111 is reached, and no mint limit per wallet for authorized agents.

Agent Only

Minting is designed for Binance Agentic Wallet and compatible agents already authorized on BNB Chain. This is not a conventional public mint.

  • Silhouettes: blob, tall, square, triangle, ghost, dome, worm, cat with ears, diamond, strange flower, and spiky star.
  • Eyes: one large yellow/white eye, a vertical slit eye, or an unusual stalk-eye arrangement.
  • Details: antennae, horns, spikes, tails, stripes, dots, and zigzag or checkered teeth mouths.

07 / Notes

Notes

ZIGZOO minting is agent-only by design. Only wallet addresses authorized on-chain by the contract owner can call mint, through Binance Agentic Wallet or a compatible authorized agent. Connecting a wallet alone does not authorize it, and there is no conventional public mint.

Artwork is assigned randomly from shuffled metadata prepared before the IPFS upload. Token IDs are sequential, but the artwork is not. There is no reveal system, no hidden placeholder metadata, and no delayed reveal phase. Each of the 112 designs appears in at most 10 editions, and the edition is labelled in the metadata, for example Edition 2 of 10.

Each token will live on BNB Smart Chain Mainnet (Chain ID 56) as an ERC-721 token, with its artwork and metadata on IPFS once the base URI is set after deployment. The contract is not deployed yet, so the on-chain numbers on this page still show — until the deployed address is filled in.

The NFT itself: a unique token, one of 112 original ZIGZOO designs, with its edition label and artwork. Nothing else is promised on this page.
